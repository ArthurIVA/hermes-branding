Hermes Agent

Self-hosted personal AI assistant

Hermes Agent is a personal AI assistant that the owner runs on his own hardware at home. This page exists as the required application home page for the Google OAuth consent screen that the assistant uses to read and send the owner's own Gmail, and to read the owner's own Calendar, Drive, Contacts, Sheets and Docs.

What this application does

  • Runs locally on the owner's own machines — no third-party AI service receives his data by default.
  • Reads and sends the owner's own Gmail, and reads his Calendar, Drive, Contacts, Sheets and Docs, so it can summarize his inbox, draft replies he approves, report on his schedule, and update his own spreadsheets.
  • Performs scheduled jobs for the owner only — for example a morning email summary and recurring work reports.

This application is not published, marketed or distributed. It serves one Google account: its owner's. No other user is asked to sign in.

Scopes requested and why

  • Gmail read / send / modify — inbox triage, summaries, and sending the reports and replies the owner asks for.
  • Calendar — daily schedule awareness and meeting prep.
  • Drive / Docs / Sheets — reading and updating the owner's own documents and spreadsheets (including a shared household budget sheet).
  • Contacts (read only) — resolving names for email recipients.
