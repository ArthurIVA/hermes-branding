Privacy Policy

Hermes Agent — last updated 2026-11-19

This application is a personal, self-hosted AI assistant used by a single person: its developer and owner, Arthur Wisner (IV-A). It is not offered as a service to the public, and it does not collect data about any other individual. This policy describes how the owner's own Google account data is handled.

Who controls the data

The owner is both the application developer and the only user. All data processed by this application is his own personal data, processed on hardware he owns and administers.

What Google data is accessed, and why

  • Gmail (read, send, modify): to summarize the owner's inbox, search his mail, draft replies for his review, and send reports he has asked the assistant to produce.
  • Calendar: to see his schedule and prepare him for meetings.
  • Drive, Docs, Sheets: to read and update his own files, including a shared household budget spreadsheet.
  • Contacts (read only): to resolve display names for email recipients.

Where the data goes

The application runs on the owner's private home network. Model inference is performed locally on his own hardware by default. When he explicitly chooses a cloud model provider, prompt text derived from his own data may be sent to that provider for inference; this is an opt-in escalation he initiates, not the default. Google API responses are processed in memory and stored only in the owner's private workspace files on his own machines.

What is not done

  • No selling, renting, sharing, or advertising of any data — to anyone, for any purpose.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, cookies, or telemetry are embedded in this application or its pages.
  • No other user's Google account is accessed. No third party is asked to authorize this application.
  • No data is retained on third-party servers by this application; the assistant stores working notes and logs locally.

Security measures

OAuth refresh tokens are stored in a permission-restricted file (mode 0600) on the owner's private machine, inside a virtual machine on his home LAN. Credentials for other systems are kept in a password manager. The assistant does not log message bodies to any external service.

Retention and deletion

The owner may revoke this application's access at any time from his Google Account security settings, which immediately ends all API access. Local notes and logs created by the assistant can be deleted by him at any time; they live only on his own storage.

Children

This application is not directed at children and is used solely by its adult owner.

Contact

Any question about this policy should be directed to the owner, Arthur Wisner (IV-A).
